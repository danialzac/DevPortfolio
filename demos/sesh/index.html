Gear

What to bring

We do not lend gloves. Shared gloves stay wet, they smell, and they pass on skin infections between strangers. Gloves are personal kit, like running shoes.

You bring

  • Water and a towel
  • Shoes you can move in
  • Your own gloves and wraps, once you have them

The coach brings

  • Pads and mitts
  • Bands and a mat
  • The session plan

No gloves yet? Start anyway.

Pick the no-glove option when you book and the session is built around things gloves are not needed for — stance, guard, footwork, shadow work, pad-free drills and conditioning. That is where a beginner should be spending the first few hours regardless.

A glove-free session covers

  • 01Stance, guard and footwork
  • 02Shadow work and combinations in the air
  • 03Kicks and knees on the coach's pads using shins, not hands
  • 04Conditioning, circuits and strength work
Book without gloves

When you are ready to buy

14oz or 16oz boxing gloves and a pair of 4m hand wraps will cover everything here. Expect to pay around $60 to $120 for a pair that lasts. Ask before you buy and we will point you at the right size — the wrong gloves are worse than none.
